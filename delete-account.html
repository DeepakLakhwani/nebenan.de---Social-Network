How to Delete Your Account

We respect your privacy and provide a way for you to delete your account and request deletion of your associated personal data. You can delete your account directly through the app or contact our support team if you cannot access the app.

Option 1: Delete Your Account in the App

  1. Open the nebenan.de : social network app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open Account Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm your request.

After your deletion request is confirmed, your account will be processed for deletion according to our account deletion procedures.

Option 2: Request Account Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team. Please use the email address associated with your account, if possible, and include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line.

What Data May Be Deleted?

Depending on the features you use, account deletion may include your account profile, personal information, posts, photos, comments, and other content associated with your account, to the extent applicable to our services and deletion procedures.

Data Retention

Certain information may be retained where required or permitted by applicable law, or when necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Such information will be retained only for the period and purposes permitted or required.

Deletion Requests and Support

Our support team may contact you if additional information is reasonably needed to verify your request and protect your account. Please do not send your password or other sensitive login credentials by email.
